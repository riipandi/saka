## Contracts the screens encode

- **Show-once everywhere** — webhook secrets and API keys mint and
  rotate through `ShowOnceSecretDialog`; no view re-reads a raw
  credential, ever.
- **adminOnly gating** — webhooks and the all-keys oversight are gated
  by role (`isAdministrator`), not a catalog slug: the guard serves
  them by the `Admin` rule and neither resource exists in
  `internal/authz/catalog.go`. Every other admin item keeps its slug.
- **Streaming semantics** — the bell, the toasts, and the inbox share
  one stream; events invalidate the unread count and the inbox list;
  reconnects are exponential with jitter; `ping` is swallowed.
- **Bigint literals** — proto int64 arrives as bigint; every runtime
  comparison spells `BigInt(0)`/`BigInt(99)`. In browser-test-processed
  modules a `2n > 0n` literal mis-compiles to false.
- **Renewal is state-gated in the UI** — the Renew control renders only
  for expired, unrevoked keys; the backend's `failed_precondition` for
  live keys is contract, not an error path to design around.
