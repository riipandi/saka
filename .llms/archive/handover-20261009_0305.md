## Decisions worth remembering

- **One store for every provider** (owner's pick, studied against Clerk):
  `oauth_connections` holds builtin AND custom credentials (sealed `enc:`),
  the CRUD is the single management surface, the config file's only SSO
  fact is `oauth.enabled`. The `env:`-interpolated config credentials the
  owner first sketched were dropped with this decision; a bootstrap/IaC
  seeding path is future work.
- The settings prefix normalized: `oauthsso.account_linking_enabled` →
  `oauth.account_linking_enabled`.
- The reserved surface prefixes (`web/binding.go` `surfacePrefixes`) are a
  hard boundary for SPA route names — anything under `/oauth`, `/api`,
  `/rpc`, `/oidc`, `/storage`, `/metrics`, `/.well-known`, `/debug` answers
  the JSON envelope, never the SPA shell. Check this list before naming an
  SPA route.
