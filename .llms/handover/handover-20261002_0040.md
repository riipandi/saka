## The preference document contract

- Storage: `users.metadata` JSONB, NULL when no key rests. Go contract: the
  closed `user.UserMetadata` struct (`modules/identity/user/schema.go`) —
  locale + timezone are the whole document; `ParseUserMetadata` answers the
  zero document on absent or malformed bytes; `Raw` rests no bytes when no
  key is set.
- Wire: `User.metadata` (`UserMetadata` message) answers the block; the
  requests keep their flat, validated fields; the IANA-zone check stays in
  `normalizeTimezone`.
- Defaults: an absent document or key answers `UTC` / unset — a corrupt
  document degrades the presentation, never the account read. Seeded and
  bootstrap accounts write no document.
- A new preference is a struct field and a proto field — never a migration.

## Owner decisions settled here

- Migration order: authorization third, multifactor fourth, webauthn fifth,
  settings sixth; federation last.
- `user_mfa_pending` renamed with its siblings for consistency.
- Residue cleanup aggressive: tables, functions, and indexes with no reader
  go; `users.metadata` and its GIN index stay (and are now load-bearing);
  `idx_api_keys_last_used_at` stays — the list sorts by it.
- Token tables stay separate: `auth_tokens`, `signup_tokens`, and
  `device_login_requests` are three lifecycles — merging was weighed and
  refused.
- Plan docs carry frontmatter (`status`, `updated`, `phases` with
  `commits`), a test matrix, and a final E2E/docs/handover phase.
