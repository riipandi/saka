1. `5474a914` — **the bucket is the backend container**. The `storage_buckets` row's name is

   dev compose provisions. Details: decision 20 in the plan, `internal/storage/README.md`.
2. `97ab52e8` — **timezone unified**: `database.timezone` and `queue.timezone` became

   session `RuntimeParams`, the scheduler's cron zone, and the migration command's pool
   options — all read `c.App.Timezone` now.
