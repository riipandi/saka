### Defects fixed along the way (last session)

- `audit.NewRecorder(nil)` did not actually discard — its failure path called a nil logger and panicked. Now falls back to `slog.DiscardHandler` (`internal/audit/recorder.go`).
- Audit `resource_id` is a UUID column: handlers must record the raw UUID, never the wire TypeID (`que_…`). `queue.UUIDFromWire` / `scheduler.UUIDFromWire` convert wire → raw.
