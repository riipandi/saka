## Behavior notes worth remembering

- The end-session hint's subject is accepted in both the raw UUID form
  (what the provider mints into `sub`) and the wire form; a pairwise
  subject would fail the lookup and be refused.
- The JOSE type member — not a token claim — is the discriminator:
  access tokens mint with `at+jwt` (RFC 9068), ID tokens mint with
  `typ: JWT` (the signing path sets it), and the policy refuses an
  `at+jwt` hint. `saka:token_type` remains a reserved custom-claim key
  only.
- `oidc.end_session_revokes_consent` (default off) decides whether a
  successful logout also drops the authorized-client ledger, forcing
  re-consent on the next sign-in.
- The device verification GET with a signed-in browser completes the
  flow outright for a consent-skipping client — a 200 with an empty body
  is success, not a stuck prompt.
- `key:generate --env-file` on an existing file needs `--overwrite`;
  without it the command asks interactively and a scripted run silently
  writes nothing.
