## The two product fixes the E2E ladder caught

1. **`publicOnly` discarded the presented caller.** `internal/transport/middleware/bearer.go`
   excused public procedures from authentication entirely, so a session bearer on a
   public procedure never reached the handler. The MFA enrollment's caller is
   "a session, or the `mfa.required` bridge" — the session half was unreachable:
   enrolling a TOTP from the settings page answered 401 forever. The middleware now
   authenticates best-effort on public procedures: a presented bearer attaches its
   caller, an absent one still answers the public refusal.
2. **The multifactor issuer seam read through the password join.** `FindAccountByID`
   inner-joins `user_passwords` — every passwordless account (which is what a JIT
   account is) was invisible to its own enrollment, to the bridge's completion, and
   to the recovery-code reads. The seam now names `FindAccountByIDAny`; the two reads
   webauthn keeps (inner-join for stranding, any for existence) stay the pattern.

Both fixes are covered by the existing suites; the ladder proves them over the wire.

## The E2E mock: navikt/mock-oauth2-server

`container/compose-dev.yaml` runs ONE `mock-oauth2-server` container carrying both
simulations as issuerIds — `/google` (port 3220) and `/github` (port 3220) — behind an
nginx TLS front (`container/nginx-proxy.conf`, the mkcert pair from `task cert:generate`).
The identity comes from the login page's username alone, through the subject-prefix
mappings in `container/mock-oauth2.json` (`JSON_CONFIG_PATH`):

- `alice` — verified address with names: the JIT, binding, linking, and MFA branches
- `unverified-*` — `email_verified: false`: the email-code gate
- `nonames-*` — no given/family claims: the continue step

Two details that took the longest to find:

- **The proxy must send `X-Forwarded-Proto`/`X-Forwarded-Port`.** The mock derives its
  issuer URLs from those headers; without them the discovery document (and every
  id_token's `iss`) is `http://` — which the feature's https enforcement refuses.
- **nginx resolves `proxy_pass` hostnames once.** After recreating the upstream
  container, restart `mock-tls` or the browser lands on a dead IP wearing an express
  error page.

`ngauth/server` was tried first and abandoned: its `generateIdToken` signs with
`expiresIn` over claims that already carry `exp`, and jsonwebtoken refuses the double —
no published version can mint an id_token at all.

The ladder — `tools/e2e-oauthsso/ladder.py`, 24 wire-level checks against the release
binary (serve with `--config-file` carrying a raised `rate_limit`, or the credential
bucket trips at 10/min) — covers: JIT with the names pause, the binding sign-in, the
verified-email link, the unverified gate (the code read from Mailpit's `/api/v1`),
the nonames continue, the stranding refusal behind the step-up gate (the
reauthentication email code is how a passwordless account proves a step-up), the MFA
bridge after OAuth, the invite-mode refusal, and the same-turn cleanup. It passed
24/24; rows it created were removed and the server stopped in the same turn.
