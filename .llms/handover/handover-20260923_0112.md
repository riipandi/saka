## Decisions worth not relitigating

- Transport is `resty.dev/v3` **v3.0.0-rc.4**. Retry defaults in Resty do not cover 5xx or non-temporary network errors; those conditions live in `internal/fetcher/error.go`. POST and PATCH are not retried. `retry_count` is capped at 5. Breaker failure threshold must exceed the retry count. Reset timeout must be at least one attempt timeout.
- Resty's breaker is per client and `Allow()` does not receive the request, so each host (`scheme://host`) gets its own `*resty.Client` and breaker. They share one `http.Client` / transport.
- `fetcher.max_body_bytes` defaults to 16 MiB and `Validate` refuses anything above 32 MiB.
- Debug body logging and the cookie jar stay off. Redirects strip `Authorization` and `Cookie`.
- Outbound calls start a client span and inject W3C `traceparent` plus baggage. `observer.SetGlobals` installs that propagator; the OTel global default injects nothing. `internal/fetcher/trace.go` falls back to the same pair when the global propagator has no fields.
- Error text is method, scheme, host, and path. Query, userinfo, headers, and bodies are not logged. Match with `errors.Is`: `ErrNetwork`, `ErrTimeout`, `ErrCanceled`, `ErrRetryExhausted`, `ErrCircuitOpen`, `ErrStatus`.
