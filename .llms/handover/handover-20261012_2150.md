## Wire facts the screens encode

- The completion dialect is header-keyed, never bearer-keyed — the
  conformance driver reads the raw 302 (`driver.mjs`); a bearer-keyed
  capture would have broken it.
- The device entry submit can answer three shapes: the re-prompt redirect
  (unknown code), the document (bearer reaches the policy inside the
  submit request), and the bare 200 (prior consent or skip-consent).
- The decision POST answers bare 200 on the device paths (the library's
  `ConfirmationFunc`) and a redirect JSON on the authorize path.
- The interaction URL is one page, root-level, beside `device.tsx` and
  `verify-email.tsx` — the group shape the plan first sketched shrank to
  the single file (D7 note).
